These images define the visual identity for future Factory Office character and action work. Generated variants must preserve the approved proportions, outline weight, eye rendering, cel shading and warm presentation.

## Shared visual system

- polished 2D human chibi game characters;
- approximately 2.5–3 heads tall;
- clean rounded dark outlines;
- soft cel shading with warm highlights;
- expressive faces readable at mobile size;
- hands and role props slightly oversized for action clarity;
- contemporary Thai office cast;
- cream and navy shared neutrals with one dominant role color;
- production-friendly silhouettes suitable for later separated sprite parts;
- no logos, embedded UI text or permanent slot numbers in character art.

## Identity invariants

Every action sheet must keep these stable for the same character:

1. face shape and eye rendering;
2. skin tone;
3. hairstyle and role marker;
4. dominant outfit silhouette and palette;
5. body/head proportion;
6. primary role prop language;
7. core personality.

Temporary expressions, poses and secondary props may change. Slot numbers, checkmarks and status labels belong to the UI overlay or separate prop layer rather than being baked into the character body asset.
